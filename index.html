FAQ

Questions clients usually ask

Something else on your mind? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Can you fix my existing website?

Yes. Send me the website link and a short note or screenshot of the problem. I find the cause, tell you what needs fixing, and then fix it on WordPress, Shopify or custom-coded sites, even if someone else built it.

How long does a website take?

Most business websites are ready in 1 to 3 weeks. Shopify stores and custom React or Angular apps take longer depending on features. You get a clear timeline before work starts.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I've built websites for clients in the UK, Dubai and India. We talk on WhatsApp, email or video call, and I adjust to your time zone.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. WordPress and Shopify sites come with an easy dashboard, and I walk you through how to change text, images, products and pages.

Is SEO included?

On-page SEO is part of every build: page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, schema markup, sitemap and page speed.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. I help you choose a domain and hosting, connect them, set up SSL and business email, and take the site live.

What happens after launch?

I stay available for fixes, updates and new pages. Ongoing maintenance plans are available if you want regular backups, updates and speed checks.
